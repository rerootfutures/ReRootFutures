Who we are

Re Root Futures supports neurodivergent young people, and also students who have no diagnosis but have behaviour needs, find school tough or have things going on at home. With or without an EHC plan, they are welcome here.

School is not only about maths and English. We find different ways for young people to learn, have fun, build confidence and discover what they are good at.

What we do

1

Mentoring Services

Meeting young people where they are and catering to their current needs, one to one and in groups.

2

Pathway and Purpose Services

Empowering young people to turn their potential into possibilities, through four programmes:

Taking impact beyond the classroom, and building a community that cares and caters for young people with neurodivergence.

Working with schools

A 4-week programme, and the young people choose what we do.

  1. Week 1: Discovery DayWe get to know each other and I find out what they are good at. They meet the four programmes and vote for what they want to do.
  2. Weeks 2 to 4: Your programmeThree sessions, one a week, built around what the young people chose.
  3. After week 4: Keep goingIf the school and students love it, we continue and grow.

My story

I’m Maryam Ali, founder and director of Re Root Futures. I’m dyslexic, and secondary school was really tough for me.

I was diagnosed at school. That was when one teacher really saw what was going on. They supported me and helped me through the years, and that support helped me so much. I have never forgotten it.

Since I was 18, I have worked in schools, in mentoring, sports leading and speech and language support, with young people with many different disabilities. I have seen how much it changes things when one adult sees a young person for who they are.

That is why I started Re Root Futures: so more young people have someone who sees them, listens and backs them, and who helps them find the way that works for them. Being on a different path does not mean being behind.

Why Re Root Futures

A young person who feels behind needs a different route, not lower expectations. We help them put down roots, grow in their own way and build a future that fits them.

Our vision

To make a real difference for young people, and to help neurodivergent young people feel that school can work for them. Maths and English are not the only way to succeed. There are other ways, and we help them find theirs.

Questions

Do young people need a diagnosis?

No. We support neurodivergent young people and students without a diagnosis, with or without an EHC plan.

Where do the sessions happen?

In your school or community space. I work around the time you can offer, and sessions are flexible in length.

How big are the groups?

Small groups of up to 12 to 15 students, so every young person is seen and heard.

How do students choose what to do?

At the Discovery Day in week 1 they meet all four programmes and vote for what they want to do.

Are you DBS checked?

Yes, I hold a DBS check. I will also complete safeguarding training and have risk assessments in place before any programme starts.
